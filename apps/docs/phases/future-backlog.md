# Future Sandbox & Feature Backlog

> **Status:** 💡 Concept / Exploratory Backlog  
> **Goal:** Track innovative ideas and advanced concepts to evaluate after the core trust loop is proven at national scale.

---

## Sandbox Concepts

- [ ] **Emergency Rapid-Response Protocol:** Fast-track review queue for natural disasters or urgent community crises.
- [ ] **AI-Assisted Evidence Pre-Check:** Automated image checks on milestone photos (verifying location EXIF data, construction authenticity, or invoice OCR text).
- [ ] **Impact Badges NFT / On-Chain Audit Proof:** Optional digital certificates of contribution for institutional donors.
- [ ] **Micro-Grant Pool:** Community-voted micro-grant allocations for top-rated local proposals.
- [ ] **Offline-First Volunteer Mobile App:** Mobile PWA allowing on-ground volunteers to capture photos and evidence without active internet connectivity.
