# Phase 2 — Regional Growth & Local Networks

> **Status:** 📋 Planned  
> **Goal:** Strengthen repeat participation, expand local community presence, introduce trusted community reviewers, and enable recurring support.

---

## 1. Overview

Phase 2 scales Sharity beyond initial curated pilots. It introduces community governance mechanisms to decentralize milestone reviews, dedicated city/neighborhood hub pages, material contribution fulfillment, and NGO partner tools.

---

## 2. Key Modules & Checklists

### 2.1 Trusted Community Reviewer Network
- [ ] Reviewer application and onboarding workflow.
- [ ] Peer-review portal for vetted community verifiers to evaluate milestone evidence.
- [ ] Reputation scoring model for verifiers based on consistency and moderation agreement.

### 2.2 City & Neighborhood Hubs
- [ ] City landing pages (e.g. Pune, Bengaluru, Mumbai).
- [ ] Regional impact metrics (total projects completed, active volunteers, total community funding).
- [ ] Community leaderboards celebrating top local outcomes.

### 2.3 Enhanced Contribution Modes
- [ ] Recurring monthly support preferences for verified cause categories or local areas.
- [ ] Direct material/items fulfillment tracking (shipping integration or local drop-off points).
- [ ] Advanced volunteer shift scheduling and attendance confirmation.

### 2.4 NGO & Partner Portals
- [ ] Organization profile verification badges.
- [ ] Multi-project dashboard for registered non-profits.
- [ ] Beneficiary privacy controls for sensitive medical or personal assistance projects.
